Research Rules

  1. Start with hyperscaler capex and capacity commentary.
  2. Confirm bottlenecks through backlog, lead times, orders, and margins.
  3. Prefer lagging suppliers where estimates are rising faster than the stock.
  4. Avoid names where valuation already assumes perfect execution.
  5. Re-score after every earnings report or major capex update.
